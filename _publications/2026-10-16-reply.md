   In response to [our paper](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2520832123) about the effects of response biases on correlations between confidence and self-reported psychiatric tendencies, [Claire](https://gillanlab.com/clairegillan/), [Steve](https://profiles.ucl.ac.uk/28069-steve-fleming), [Marion](https://marionrouault.weebly.com/) and [Tricia](https://seowxft.github.io/) published [a response](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2624329123). We were invited to respond to the response, which we did here (mostly re-iterating points we made in the [supplementary materials](https://www.pnas.org/doi/suppl/10.1073/pnas.2520832123/suppl_file/pnas.2520832123.sapp.pdf), pp. 49-57).
